# Outreach angle: reaching real architects/builders (draft, not posted)

Per standing instruction: no "indie"/consumer framing, no posting from here — owner's account
login required for all of these. This is a ready draft of **where** and **how**, not an
auto-post.

## Where architects/builders actually are (not generic social media)

- **AIA (American Institute of Architects) Knowledgenet communities** — the real professional
  home base for US licensed architects; technology/practice-management forums specifically
  discuss AI design tools. Requires AIA membership to post (owner-gated).
- **Archinect forums** (`archinect.com/forum`) — the biggest open architecture-practitioner
  forum, genuinely technical crowd, existing threads comparing Maket.ai/Finch/Hypar. No login
  wall to read; posting needs an account (owner-gated).
- **r/architecture** and **r/ArchitecturalRevit** (Reddit) — mixed student/practitioner but a
  real chunk of licensed architects post there; r/ArchitecturalRevit specifically skews
  professional/BIM-literate, a good fit for the IFC-export angle.
- **r/Construction** and **r/HomeBuilding** — real builders/GCs, a good fit for the ADU and
  multi-story test cases (less BIM-literate audience, more "can I actually build from this"
  framing).
- **LinkedIn**: AEC-specific groups ("Architecture & Engineering professionals," "AIA" company
  page followers, "ADU" and "small residential design-build" hashtag communities) — the
  BRANDING-MASTERCLASS playbook's standing "founder-led + brand-led" posting pattern applies
  here directly; LinkedIn is where small-firm principals and builders who make purchasing
  decisions actually are, more than Twitter/X for this specific audience.
- **Houzz Pro community** / **NAHB (National Association of Home Builders) forums** — reaches
  builders and remodelers directly, a good fit for the ADU case specifically (ADUs are an NAHB
  hot topic in 2026).
- **ArchDaily** comment sections on AI-design-tool articles — lower-effort, high-visibility if a
  relevant article is already live; reactive, not a dedicated post.

## Angle (honest, technical-first, not hype)

Lead with the finding, not the product: *"Tested HomePlan against Maket.ai's published feature
set on 3 real briefs (multi-story family home, ADU, L-shaped courtyard) — here's exactly what
passed, what got flagged, and the real PDF/DXF/IFC files it produced."* Technical credibility >
consumer marketing for this audience: show the dimensioned room table and the IFC file parsing
cleanly, not a hero shot.

Suggested post skeleton (English, draft only — adapt per channel's norms, Archinect/AIA want
more technical depth than LinkedIn):

> Ran 3 real briefs through HomePlan (open-source, MIT, self-hostable) and checked them against
> Maket.ai's and ARCHITEChTURES' published feature sets — not a hype comparison, a real one.
> Multi-story family home, an ADU, and an L-shaped courtyard home with a 3-car garage. Every
> output comes with numbered structural warnings instead of a silent bad layout — real PDF/DXF/
> IFC exports from all three, linked below. Also found and am logging two real gaps (an ADU
> sizing issue, a 2-story primary-bath aspect-ratio issue) instead of hiding them. Full writeup
> + real files: [link]. Free self-hosted, MIT licensed: github.com/caelum0x/homeplan
