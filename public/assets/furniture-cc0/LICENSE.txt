Files in this directory (renamed from Kenney's original GLTF-format filenames,
geometry/materials unmodified):

  sofa.glb   <- Furniture Kit "loungeSofa.glb"
  bed.glb    <- Furniture Kit "bedDouble.glb"
  table.glb  <- Furniture Kit "table.glb"
  chair.glb  <- Furniture Kit "chair.glb"
  desk.glb   <- Furniture Kit "desk.glb"

Vendored 2026-11-04 as part of HomePlan's furniture-realism pass (see
plans/homeplan.md and lib/furnitureAssets.ts). All 5 meshes are wired into
the live 3D view (components/FloorPlan3D.tsx): sofa.glb -> sofa, bed.glb ->
bed_queen, table.glb -> dining_table, chair.glb -> dining_chair, desk.glb ->
desk.

Other known CC0 sources evaluated for this task (not used here, documented for
the next person who extends this set):
  - Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza) -- mirrors Kenney + Quaternius CC0 packs as
    individual downloadable glTF/GLB models, no account required.
  - Kenney.nl itself (https://kenney.nl/assets) -- dozens of other CC0 kits
    (City, Isometric Rooms, Mini Dungeon, etc.) in the same GLTF-format/.glb
    layout as this one.
  - Quaternius (https://quaternius.com, mirrored on Poly Pizza and
    OpenGameArt.org under "All CC0 - Uploader: quaternius") -- CC0 1.0 public
    domain, "Ultimate House Interior Pack" and a dedicated low-poly furniture
    pack cover the same sofa/bed/table/chair/desk categories at a different
    art style (more stylized/rounded than Kenney's kit).
  - OpenGameArt.org -- hosts mirrors of both the Kenney and Quaternius CC0
    packs above under its own CC0 filter.
  - Sketchfab's CC0 filter -- higher-poly individual furniture models exist
    there under CC0, but (unlike the two packs above) each model's license
    has to be verified per-upload rather than trusting one pack-level LICENSE
    file, so it was not used for this first pass.
